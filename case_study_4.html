Design Intent

  • It is a 2D battle-royale simulation, a real-time strategy game where a player has to face-off against other 4 creatures and be the last one to survive. The players have to mutate their forms, craft items, or kill wild monsters in hopes of growing stronger. The player will traverse multiple areas in the environment, mutate to grow stronger to guarantee their survival. With branching mutations of endless possibilities, the player can create their own survival playstyle and be the last to survive. This serves as my exploration into a fusion of real-time and turn-based games.

Constraints

  • For this project, I had a clear understanding that time management was important. I was doing a project meant to be worked in groups of two, so I had my work cut out for me. I made sure to space out my tasks well and complete the game within the timeframe given. I enjoy having games that give the players the freedom of choice, so I did my best to apply it here!
